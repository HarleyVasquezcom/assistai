assistai

reads the page and answers locally

No network calls, no API keys, no models in the cloud. assistai reads the page you are on and replies with deterministic local heuristics — summary by word density, keywords with counts, entities by regex — plus a persistent conversation history.

Summary by word density Keywords with counts Emails, URLs, prices, dates 6 languages Conversation stays on your machine
Download ZIP assistai.zip — unpack, load unpacked in chrome://extensions

What it does

  • Type a command — “resumen”, “keywords”, “entities”, “clear” — or use the quick chips in the popup.
  • A brief: the three densest sentences of the page, picked by repeated key terms.
  • Keywords: the page’s most repeated content words, with counts.
  • Entities: emails, URLs, prices and ISO dates found by regex.
  • Unknown commands get an honest “I don’t understand” — no invented answers.
  • Everything stays in extension storage; nothing leaves the browser.

Install

  • Download assistai.zip and unpack it somewhere permanent.
  • Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode.
  • Click “Load unpacked” and pick the folder (keep the folder after loading).
  • Open any page, open the assistai popup, and ask.
